## Predictions (snapshot 1, 14 Oct, page 1 = 33 jams: 10 banned_all, 8 banned_assets,
6 allowed_disclose, 1 required, 8 not_stated; pages 1+2: 36 of 56 stated jams banned assets)
- P1: Of the stated game jams, >= 55% ban AI-made assets in some form
  (banned_all + banned_most + banned_assets, plus mixed jams that ban in their main track).
- P2: At most 8 of the 44 are allowed, allowed_disclose, required or required_tool combined.
- P3: not_stated is between 20% and 45% of the 44.
- P4: No jam that is in both snapshot 1 page 1 and this sample changed from a ban class to
  an allow class (relaxations = 0).
- P5: Among jams with >= 1,000 joined, none is `allowed` (AI without restriction).
- P6: At most 3 of the 44 offer stated cash prizes AND allow AI-made assets
  (allowed / allowed_disclose / required, no score penalty).

## What would count as a surprise worth reporting
Any relaxation (P4 fails), or the stated-ban share dropping below 55% (P1 fails).
