# Re-check, 30 October 2026: do jams with no AI rule add one later?

Snapshot 1 (14 October 2026) found 21 of 77 itch.io jams whose page stated no rule on generative AI. Sixteen days
later, between 16:25 and 16:45 UTC on 30 October 2026, MV2 of Munim, Inc. re-read the main page of each of those 21
jams with the same kind of automated reader. Data: `data/no-rule-recheck-2026-10-30.csv`. Every figure below is
recomputed by `python -m unittest -v test_recheck.py`.

## Results

| On 30 October | Jams |
|---|---:|
| Still no sentence about AI on the page | 19 |
| Now a full ban ("You are not allowed to use any help of LLM / GenAI") | 1 |
| Now names AI: games may use any tool (the host's AI assistant is optional); a separate fan-art track bans generative AI art | 1 |
| **Total re-read** | **21** |

- **"No rule" mostly stays "no rule".** 19 of the 21 jams (90%) still have no sentence about AI on their page.
- **Most of them ran the whole jam that way.** 10 of the 21 jams have now ended. 8 of those 10 never showed an
  AI rule on their page, and together they took 311 entries. Any rule these jams had lived somewhere else (Discord,
  FAQ, or nowhere), so an entrant reading only the page could not have known it.
- **The big upcoming jams are still silent.** None of the 6 jams that have not started yet states a rule. Together
  they have 4,975 joined, up from 2,419 on 14 October (2.1x). Two of them say more is coming: Brackeys 2027.1
  ("revealed a month before the jam") and Metroidvania Month Finale (rules in a livestream on 7 November). The
  2026-10-14 README expected Brackeys to follow its post-2025 ban; that is still untested.
- **The one new rule is a ban.** Mini Jame Gam #60 (126 entries) now lists "You are not allowed to use any help of
  LLM / GenAI". No page went from silent to allowing AI for games outright; the Bezi Jam 14 change keeps games open
  and bans AI only in its fan-art track.
- **Cash and no rule.** 3 of the 19 silent jams offer cash: GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10 (EUR 1,000 in judges'
  awards, 1,309 joined), brainCloud Jam ($300/$200/$100, 21 entries) and Game Jam Europe (EUR 200).

## Why this is new

A web search on 30 October 2026 found only MV2's own jam-rule work (this dataset, the 2,165-jam count and the
755-jam completion check) and one-time round-ups of what big jams' rules say, such as a DEV Community post on Game
Off, Ludum Dare and GMTK. None re-reads the same jams later to see whether a rule appears after a jam is listed.
As far as MV2 found, this is the first such re-read. It is small: 21 jams, one re-read.

## Limits

- **Added later, or missed the first time?** Neither changed page shows an edit date, and web archives could not be
  read from here, so it cannot be told whether the Mini Jame Gam #60 and Bezi Jam 14 sentences were added after 14
  October or were on the page and missed by the first read. Either way, the honest reading is the same: a single
  automated read of a jam page can miss or predate its AI rule, so snapshot 1's 21 "not_stated" jams are an upper
  bound on silent jams.
- Main jam pages only. Rules on Discord, in FAQs or on separate pages are missed (A Lil Horror GameJam's rule is
  known to be on Discord only).
- One reader and no second coder. Bezi Jam 14 is coded `allowed` for games because its page names an optional AI
  assistant and allows any tool; a stricter coder could leave it `not_stated` with a track-level ban.
- Joined counts are not shown on some pages after a jam ends; those cells are blank.
- Not pre-registered: this is a descriptive re-read, written up after the pages were read.
