## What was done
On 14 November 2026 (about 02:00-02:30 UTC) each of the 21 jams labelled `not_stated` in the 14 October snapshots
(`data/itch-jams-2026-10-14.csv` and `data/itch-jams-2026-10-14-page2.csv`) was re-read once, main page only, asking
for every sentence about AI, generative tools or tool/asset rules. Predictions were written down first in
[PREREGISTRATION-notstated-recheck-2026-11-14.md](PREREGISTRATION-notstated-recheck-2026-11-14.md). That file
was written without knowing that the 30 October re-check existed, so one of its "new" findings (Mini Jame Gam #60)

## Predictions, graded as written
- P1 (at least 2 of 3 "rules later" jams now state a rule): **failed**, 1 of 3 (Metroidvania Month 34). Game Off
  2026 opened with no AI sentence on its page; Brackeys 2027.1 has not posted its full page yet.
- P2 (at most 2 of 18 others now state a rule): **held**, 1 of 18 (Mini Jame Gam #60, already known on 30 Oct).
- P3 (if Game Off 2026 states a rule it allows AI): **not testable on the page**, which states none. Its
  off-page rule does allow AI, which is what P3 expected, but P3 was about the page and is not counted as held.
- P4 (no page moves to `required`/`required_tool`): **held**.

## What this adds
Across two re-reads a month apart, 2 of 21 silent jam pages (10%) gained a rule, and both were bans; none gained a
permission. A silent page stayed silent in 19 of 21 cases, including three jams that are open right now. But a
silent page is not proof that no rule exists: the biggest of the 21, Game Off 2026, has its rule only in the
organiser's blog post. An entrant should look for the host's announcement, FAQ or Discord before assuming that a
silent page means anything goes. We found no other study that re-reads the same jam pages over time.

## Limits
One reader, one fetch per page, through a tool that summarises pages, so a sentence could be missed (each quote
above was asked for verbatim). Hosts can edit pages at any time and itch.io shows no edit dates, so a "new" rule
may have been missed earlier. Main pages only: off-page rules such as Game Off's were not looked for
systematically, and the Game Off one was found afterwards, by a web search. 21 jams, not a random sample. Joined
counts are not shown on ended jam pages.
